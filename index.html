How it works

  1. Sign in with visa details — we verify your eligibility (RBI-compliant KYC flow).
  2. We create a temporary virtual debit card loaded in INR at official exchange rates.
  3. Use the card for local payments or collect cash from partner banks.

Key features

  • Visa-based secure login & verification
  • Temporary virtual debit cards (INR)
  • Automatic conversion from your home currency at official rates
  • Cash collection from partnered banks
  • Transaction history and spending controls
